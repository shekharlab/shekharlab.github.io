Thank you for your interest in joining our group. Please read this page before writing to me. It will help you decide whether we are a good fit, and it will help me respond to you.

## Is our group right for you?

We are primarily a theory and computation group. We do carry out some experiments, but they are not our mainstay, and most of our work involves developing models, analyzing data, and writing code. If you are primarily an experimentalist, we may not be a good fit for you at present. Our current research is described on our [research page]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}).

## Prospective PhD students

I cannot admit students directly. To join the group, you must first be admitted to one of Berkeley's PhD programs. Our group is affiliated with:

- [Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering](https://chemistry.berkeley.edu/cbe)
- [Computational Biology](https://ccb.berkeley.edu)
- [Neuroscience (Helen Wills Neuroscience Institute)](https://hwni.berkeley.edu/home)
- [Biophysics](https://qb3.berkeley.edu/biophysics/)
- [Vision Science (School of Optometry)](https://vision.berkeley.edu/about-us/vision-science-program/)

If you think you would be a good fit for our group, please email me at [kshekhar@berkeley.edu](mailto:kshekhar@berkeley.edu) with your CV and a short statement of your research interests. Before you write:

- Read our [research page]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) and at least one of our [recent papers]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}).
- Tell me about your background and training, and the skills you would bring.
- Tell me which paper you read and what you took away from it.

**A note on application season.** Every October and November, around the PhD application deadlines, I receive a deluge of emails from prospective students expressing interest in the group. I normally do not respond to these unless it is absolutely clear that the writer has a specific interest in our research and has read our papers. If you are coming across our group during this period, kindly forgive me for not replying. If you think you would be a good fit, please apply to one of the graduate programs listed above, indicate my name in your application, and then send me an email telling me specifically why you are interested in our group.

## Prospective postdocs

I am always glad to hear from postdoctoral candidates whose interests overlap with ours, particularly those with strong quantitative training. Please email me at [kshekhar@berkeley.edu](mailto:kshekhar@berkeley.edu) with your CV and the names of two or three references. Before you write:

- Read our [research page]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) and at least one of our [recent papers]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}).
- Tell me about your background and training, and the skills you would bring.
- Tell me which paper you read and what you took away from it.
- Describe one or two directions you would like to pursue in our group.

I am also happy to support strong candidates applying for independent postdoctoral fellowships, as long as there is a genuine alignment in scientific interests and values.

## Undergraduates

I can advise only undergraduates currently enrolled at UC Berkeley who are strongly motivated by the areas of our research. I am unable to advise students remotely, and I do not have the capacity to host summer or visiting internships.

If you are a Berkeley undergraduate interested in working with us, before you write:

- Read our [research page]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) and try reading one of our [recent papers]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}).
- Tell me your major and year, the relevant courses you have taken, and the skills you have, such as programming or mathematics.
- Tell me which paper you read and what you took away from it. I do not expect you to have understood all the details, but I do expect that you will have attempted to read the paper carefully and will have specific questions for me.

## A note on email

Because of the large volume of email I receive, I am unable to respond to everyone individually. My apologies if I have not replied to you; please feel free to write again.

Please note, however, that I may also not respond if I do not sense a strong intellectual fit. Many of the emails I receive are generic and appear to be written by AI tools. I am much more likely to respond if you have taken the time to read our papers and your email shows that you have engaged with them, along the lines described above.

I do not expect you to know everything in advance. But I do need to sense a genuine commitment to thinking carefully and deeply about science.
